Core formulas (preview)
Distance: d(x, μ)2 = Σ(x − μ)2
Assign: ci = argminj d(xi, μj)2
Update: μj = (1/|Cj|) Σ xi ∈ Cj
Objective: J = Σ d(xi, μci)2
