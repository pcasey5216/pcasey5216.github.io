K-Means Clustering Lab

An interactive K-means “sandbox” for building intuition: click to add points, step through iterations, and use diagnostics to pick k.

  • Teaching vs Analysis mode, k-means++ or random initialization, step/auto-run
  • K-scan helpers (elbow and silhouette) plus diagnostics and exports
  • CSV upload + presets + normalization for quick experiments

Cost Estimating WBS-driven Risk / Monte Carlo v3.0.17

CASE — Cost Analysis & Simulation Engine

A browser-based estimating workflow: build a WBS, enter cost lines, apply uncertainty, and generate cost-risk outputs (including PDF/CDF “S-curve” views).

  • WBS templates + library to jump-start structuring (MIL-STD style layouts)
  • Risk distributions, sampling, and summary statistics for “what-if” exploration
  • Exports and sharing-friendly outputs for documentation and briefings

What these are

These tools are designed for education, demos, and communicating assumptions in cost-analysis contexts. They are not validated pricing engines or compliance audit instruments.
